Exploring Surveillance Capitalism

Making Art Under the Algorithm

Surveillance Capitalism operates as an infrastructure built on the systematic collection and commodification of personal data by private entities, primarily large corporations.

It perpetrates a market logic that aims to make human behaviour more visible and predictable.

For the artist, this system presents a fundamental threat: the erosion of creative potential and autonomous expression.
