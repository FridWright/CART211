Art Under the Algorithm

Creating art in the age of social media comes with a constant tension: gaining visibility while staying authentic. Algorithms favor mainstream trends, and many artists feel pressure to conform, flattening their work to gain attention.

Behavioural Nudging and Thought Control

Tech companies not only collect data on behaviour, but use that data to shape it. The subtle controls exerted by the platforms can influence human thought itself, turning users into products.

On these platforms, artists are forced to conform to algorithms just to gain an audience. This system makes information that is "safe for tech companies" easier to share.

Technological Determinism

Technological determinism argues that technology dictates social structure and cultural values, asserting an irresistible force driving human history and individual behaviour.

This viewpoint suggests that the future, and the role of data-mining within it, is inevitable. Artists must refute this viewpoint in lieu of alternate formats outside of mainstream platforms in the effort to preserve creative integrity.

...

Artists can still reach audiences without flattening their art. Using direct channels, building your community, and experimenting with unconventional formats are key strategies to maintain authenticity under algorithmic pressures.
